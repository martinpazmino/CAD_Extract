# Decision and conclusion log

Short format: decision · why · evidence · status. "Proposed" = the team still has to confirm it;
"Verified" = backed by a test in this repo.

| # | Decision | Why | Evidence | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D1 | **Vector first**: read text and paths from the CAD PDF; OCR/raster only as a fallback | Centimetre accuracy, no OCR errors | v1 and v2 exact on vector PDFs | In force |
| D2 | **Relations instead of positions**: columns from geometry, scale by regression, text by alignment | Fixed positions fail with another layout, sometimes silently | v1: A3 6/18 (7 in green), A4/B 0 %; v2: 102/102 ([01](01_diagnosis_fixed_layout.md), [02](02_bohrprofil_generalization.md)) | Verified (synthetic) |
| D3 | **Schicht boundaries come from the geometry**; the labels confirm them | Two independent signals → measurable confidence | Prototype v2, field `label_confirmed` | Verified (synthetic) |
| D4 | **Schraffur classified against the legend samples of the document itself** | No training data needed; same CAD style | 101/102 and detection of the contradiction in A5 ([04](04_schraffur_din4023.md)) | Verified (synthetic) |
| D5 | **Bodenart = main noun**; never `-ig` adjectives; no silent "continuation" | It was the cause of the green errors | A3 in v1 ([01](01_diagnosis_fixed_layout.md)) | Proposed |
| D6 | **Main KPI = silent error rate** | A visible error gets corrected; a green one reaches the model | [09](09_validation_benchmark.md) | Proposed |
| D7 | Layout profiles = **hints**, not positions | Generality without losing per-office fine-tuning | [02 §7](02_bohrprofil_generalization.md#7-what-remains-as-configuration-profile) | Proposed |
| D8 | swissgeol: **port algorithms + ground truth format, use it as an oracle, contribute upstream; no fork, no dependency on its core** | Its statistical core is robust; the German/CAD part is missing; AGPL and a beta API | 18/18 on A–A3, failures on names/water/CRS ([08](08_swissgeol_reuse.md)) | Verified (synthetic) |
| D9 | swissgeol code **reimplemented and cited**, not copied (except an isolated AGPL module) | Keep the option of a commercial PyMuPDF license open | License AGPL-3.0-only | Proposed |
| D10 | **Coordinates from tables/Lageplan/alignment** with a GOK cross-check | They unlock 3D; the Bohrprofil does not contain them | 17/17 in a synthetic table; the `tab.extract()` trap ([07](07_coordinates.md)) | Verified (table) |
| D11 | Numbers always from **words with a bbox**, never from flattened text | `find_tables().extract()` broke `565 081,64` | [07 §2](07_coordinates.md#2-verified-trap-when-reading-tables) | Verified |
| D12 | Längsschnitt: **vector polygons and colours from its legend**, tunnel by OCG layer/geometry/click | Accuracy, performance and a direct `IfcGeoslice` | Design ([03](03_laengsschnitt_generalization.md)) | Proposed |
| D13 | Sondierungen and water as **first-class data** (their own tables) | Discarded today, and they feed the model | DPH ±0.4 N10, water 17/17 ([05](05_sondierungen.md), [06](06_grundwasser.md)) | Verified (synthetic) |
| D14 | That Open: **add the geotechnical classes to `importer.classes.elements`** | By default the IfcImporter discards them | IFC spike ([architecture §8.2](ARCHITECTURE_BAUGRUNDMODELL.md#82-spike-results-verified)) | Verified |
| D15 | The LLM proposes, the geometry decides | Avoid invented numbers | [Architecture §6.9](ARCHITECTURE_BAUGRUNDMODELL.md#69-where-to-use-an-llm-and-where-not) | Proposed |
| D16 | **Test corpus from public Gutachten** (B-Pläne, UVP-Verbund, transparency portals); only manifest and ground truth in the repo, never the PDFs | No own Gutachten are available; many are published | 16 candidates in 8 Länder ([12](12_public_corpus.md)); download blocked by the environment's network | Decided (2026-10-25) |
| D17 | **Internal use first**; for third parties, decide between AGPL / Artifex license / pypdfium2 when there is a business case | Avoid premature costs and decisions | [14](14_licensing.md) | Superseded by D79 (2026-10-29) |
| D18 | **All of PyMuPDF behind our own primitives layer** | Makes switching to pypdfium2 (BSD/Apache) cheap | pypdfium2 returns the same 30 paths with style ([14 §3](14_licensing.md#3-test-can-pymupdf-be-replaced)) | Verified (synthetic) |
| D19 | **Collaborate with swisstopo**, starting with issues with synthetic reproducers and an email; contribute small pieces | Mutual benefit, low risk | Drafts in [13](13_swisstopo_collaboration.md) | Proposed (the team sends them) |
| D20 | **Geometry reduced to segments** and column = pair of stroked verticals | Regenerated PDFs (Ghostscript/PDF24) have neither rectangles nor fills | Wachenroth 18/18 only this way ([15](15_results_real_pdfs.md)) | Verified (real) |

| D26 | **Hybrid:** label detection tolerant to formats (like swissgeol) + our own geometric validation | swissgeol generalised better on depths (13/13 blind) but invented 18 Bohrungen and read no elevations; v3 the other way round | [15 §6.4](15_results_real_pdfs.md#64-consequence-for-the-architecture) | Proposed |
