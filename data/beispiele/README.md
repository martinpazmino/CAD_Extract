# Examples for the app

The step app (`python -m server`, [docs/21](../../docs/21_app_schritte_normen.md)) offers them on the
first page to go through the whole workflow without your own PDF. Only factual data or data generated
by us: third-party PDFs are **not** in the repo (source and `sha256` below).

| File | What it is | What it shows |
|---|---|---|
| [`berlin_v67b.json`](berlin_v67b.json) | Extraction v4 (canonical JSON) of the geotechnischer Bericht B-Plan V-67b-VE, Berlin: Anlage 3 as raster, read with OCR | A **georeferenced** model (EPSG:25833) with 5 Bohrungen; 2 archive Bohrungen without coordinates stay out and are marked |
| [`talheim.json`](talheim.json) | Extraction v4 of the report on the Sickerwasserleitung Talheim (vector logs) | A PDF **without coordinates**: Bohrungen in a row (own distances or dragged in the Längsschnitt), marked in the IFC and in the viewer |
| [`demo_synthetisch.json`](demo_synthetisch.json) | Synthetic case `demo` from [`baugrundmodell.synthetisch`](../../services/modeler/baugrundmodell/synthetisch.py) | 12 Bohrungen, a Kies lens, Torf that pinches out, short Bohrungen |
| [`trasse_synthetisch.json`](trasse_synthetisch.json) | Synthetic case `trasse` from the same module | **6 km Trasse**, 51 Bohrungen every 120 m: camera, boundary and performance for models > 5 km ([20](../../docs/20_lage_pruefung_viewer.md)) |
| [`synthetisch_bohrprofil.pdf`](synthetisch_bohrprofil.pdf) | PDF generated by [`synth_bohrprofil.py`](../../spikes/extraktion_v2/synth_bohrprofil.py) (layout A) | **PDF upload** end to end: extraction → model in the local coordinates of the Lageplan |

Sources of the real PDFs (not included):

- Berlin: <https://www.berlin.de/sen/stadtentwicklung/_assets/planung/bebauungsplanverfahren/bebauungsplanverfahren-der-senatsverwaltung-fuer-stadtentwicklung-bauen-und-wohnen/v-67b-ve/geotechnischer_bericht_b_plan_v67bve.pdf>, `sha256` 659713ac…27c4
- Talheim: <https://uvp-verbund.de/documents-ige-ng/igc_bw/9b935c78-6f28-4f37-9f29-2ce860316ece/230405_Geotech%20Kaiser_7867_Bericht%20Sickerwasserleitung%20Talheim.pdf>, `sha256` 50a3af26…db03

The figures for these two are **training** figures (the PDFs were used to tune the extractor,
[15](../../docs/15_results_real_pdfs.md)); they do not measure generality.
